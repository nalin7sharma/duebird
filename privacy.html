Duebird Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Duebird is an app for tracking subscriptions and recurring bills. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. Duebird has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and it does not request internet access. We do not collect, sell or share your data.

What you enter

You may enter names of subscriptions or bills, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, charge dates, categories, notes and reminder preferences. You can also add optional details: free-trial, start, pause and contract end dates, a payment label (for example a card nickname with its last 4 digits; Duebird refuses full card or account numbers), a web address for managing or cancelling the service, how many people share a plan, and an emoji or color for the item.

You can also add, if you choose: the first names of people you share a plan with, tags, whether an item is a business expense or a need or want, and, for loans, an interest rate and the amount still owed.

For the payday plan and money tools you can enter your take-home pay, payday, savings per payday, a monthly budget, a savings goal, hours worked per week, emergency savings, which apps your phone or card plan includes, and your own exchange rates. These are used only for calculations shown on your screen.

Duebird also keeps, on your device:

  • the history of price changes you make to an item, so it can show when a price went up;
  • the days you tap "Used it" on an item, your "Still using it?" answers, and which shares people have paid back;
  • receipts you attach: Android's file picker gives Duebird a copy of only the file you pick, which is kept in the app's private storage;
  • items you delete, in a recycle bin for 30 days, after which they are removed for good;
  • a record of when each reminder was delivered, used only by the Reminder check screen and deleted after 60 days.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Backups

Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Duebird's list and settings in your device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account and governed by Google's privacy policy. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings. Duebird's developer cannot access these backups.

Backup files you save. In Settings → Backup and restore you can save your list, its history, usage, receipts and settings to a file. Android's file picker asks where to save it (for example your phone's storage or a cloud drive you use), and the file is then handled by that storage under its own terms. Restoring reads only the file you pick.

Automatic backups. If you turn them on, Duebird writes a backup file once a week into a folder you pick once, keeping the newest five. Android only lets Duebird write to that folder.

Backup password. If you set one, backup files are encrypted (AES-256) with a key made from your password. The password is kept only on your device, outside Android backup, so it is never copied anywhere. Without it nobody, including Duebird's developer, can read those files.

Files you export or import

Duebird Pro can export your list as a CSV file, your coming charges as a calendar (.ics) file, and a yearly business report as CSV and PDF. You can also share a year-in-review image. These files are made on your device and only shared when you choose where to send them from Android's share sheet; once shared, they are handled by the app or person you sent them to. Duebird Pro can also import a CSV file you pick; it is read on your device and not sent anywhere.

Links you open

If you add a link for managing or cancelling a service, open a link from the cancel helper, or tap "Rate Duebird", it opens in your browser or the Play Store. Duebird itself does not connect to the internet; the website or store you visit has its own privacy policy.

Google Assistant and Quick Settings

You can ask Google Assistant to add an item or open Duebird. Assistant passes only the name you say to the app on your device; your request to Assistant is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. The Quick Settings tile shows your next charge in your phone's quick settings panel, read on your device.

App lock and hidden amounts

App lock uses Android's own fingerprint, face or screen-lock prompt. Duebird never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data, PIN, pattern or password; Android only tells it whether unlocking succeeded. While app lock is on, Duebird also keeps its content out of the recent-apps preview.

"Hide amounts" replaces amounts with dots in the app, the widget and reminders. It changes only what is displayed.

Purchases

Duebird Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Google Play handles your payment details under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy. Duebird only receives confirmation from Google Play, on your device, that Pro was purchased. We never see your payment details.

Reviews

Duebird may occasionally show Google Play's in-app review prompt. The prompt and any rating or review you leave are handled by Google Play; Duebird is not told whether you rated it or what you wrote.

Notifications

Duebird uses notifications to remind you about upcoming charges, free trials and contract end dates you entered, and, if you use those features, for the payday plan, a heads-up before an expensive month and a monthly summary. You can type a new amount into a charge reminder; it is saved on your device. All notifications are created on your device. On a secure lock screen they hide names and amounts until you unlock your phone.

Permissions

  • Notifications, to show the reminders described above.
  • Google Play billing, for the optional Pro purchase.
  • Biometric, used only when you turn on app lock.
  • Run at startup and keep the device awake briefly, so scheduled reminders survive a restart and can be delivered.

Duebird does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, calendar or storage permissions.

Children

Duebird is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete any item in the app (it is removed for good after 30 days in the recycle bin, or straight away with "Delete for good"), or uninstall Duebird to remove all of its data from your device. You can also delete the app's Android backup from your Google account settings, and delete any backup or CSV files you saved yourself wherever you stored them.
